## About

I'm a semiconductor device engineer working on advanced logic transistor technologies and their electrical performance. My interests connect **device architecture, semiconductor materials, transport physics, resistance/capacitance, and circuit-level performance**.

## Research Interests

- **Advanced logic devices:** FinFETs, GAAFETs, nanosheet FETs, and complementary FETs (CFETs)
- **Device physics:** electrostatics, carrier mobility, transport, and short-channel effects
- **Materials and integration:** Si, Ge, Ge-on-insulator, strain engineering, wafer bonding, and monolithic 3D integration
- **Electrical performance:** I–V/C–V characterization, parasitic resistance and capacitance, and DC/AC performance
- **Modeling and analysis:** semiconductor band structures, device simulation, sensitivity analysis, and physics-based performance modeling
